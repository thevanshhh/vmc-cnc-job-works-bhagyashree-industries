QUALITY VERIFICATION PROTOCOL

5-Stage Machine Acceptance Testing

01.

Bed Scraping & Leveling

Hand scraping on guideways ensuring oil pockets and flatness within 0.003 mm / 300 mm.

02.

Spindle Runout Testing

03.

Linear Laser Calibration

Multi-point laser interferometer compensation across full X, Y, and Z axis travel lengths.

04.

72-Hour Continuous Run

Endurance dry-cycle testing covering all feed rates, tool changer cycles, and coolant pumps.

Machining trials using buyer's die-steel workpiece for sign-off prior to crating and loading.
